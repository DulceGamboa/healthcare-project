MEDIPLUS — USER GUIDE
Healthcare Management System

Version: 1.0
Date: March 2026

GETTING STARTED

1. Open your browser and go to:
   http://localhost:8080/login.html

2. Click the "Registrarse" tab to create a new account.

3. Fill in your full name, email address and a password
   of at least 6 characters.

4. Click "Crear cuenta".

5. Switch to the "Iniciar sesion" tab.

6. Enter your email and password.

7. Click "Iniciar sesion".
   You will be redirected to the Dashboard automatically.

NOTE: Your session lasts 24 hours. After that you will be
asked to log in again automatically.

----------------------------------------------------------------
DASHBOARD
----------------------------------------------------------------

The Dashboard is the main screen of MediPlus. It shows:

- A welcome message with your name and time of day.
- Statistics cards showing totals for diseases, appointments,
  surgeries and medications registered in the system.
- Quick access buttons to all modules.
- AI modules section with links to Diet, Rehabilitation
  and the Virtual Assistant.
- System status indicator showing backend, database
  and authentication status.

----------------------------------------------------------------
PATIENTS
----------------------------------------------------------------

Navigate to: Sidebar -> Pacientes

To add a patient click the button that says Nuevo Paciente.
Enter the full name, email address and age of the patient.
Click Guardar Paciente and the patient will appear in the
table immediately.

To delete a patient find the row in the table and click
the Eliminar button. Confirm the action in the popup dialog.

NOTE: Patients must be registered first before you can
add diseases, appointments, surgeries, rehabilitations
or medications linked to them.

----------------------------------------------------------------
DISEASES
----------------------------------------------------------------

Navigate to: Sidebar -> Enfermedades

To add a disease click Nueva Enfermedad. Enter the name
of the condition such as Diabetes tipo 2 or Hipertension.
Select the patient from the dropdown list and click Guardar.

To delete a disease find it in the table and click Eliminar
then confirm the action.

----------------------------------------------------------------
MEDICAL APPOINTMENTS
----------------------------------------------------------------

Navigate to: Sidebar -> Citas Medicas

To add an appointment click Nueva Cita. Enter the reason
for the visit, select the appointment date and choose
a status from the following options:

    Pendiente   - appointment not yet confirmed
    Confirmada  - appointment confirmed
    Completada  - appointment already attended
    Cancelada   - appointment was cancelled

Then select the patient from the dropdown and click
Guardar Cita.

At the top of the page there is a statistics bar showing
the total count of appointments broken down by status:
Total, Pending, Completed and Cancelled.

To delete an appointment find it in the table, click
Eliminar and confirm.

----------------------------------------------------------------
SURGERIES
----------------------------------------------------------------

Navigate to: Sidebar -> Cirugias

To add a surgery click Nueva Cirugia. Enter the type of
surgery such as Apendicectomia or Rodilla derecha. Select
the surgery date and enter any clinical notes if needed.
Select the patient from the dropdown and click Guardar Cirugia.

To delete a surgery find it in the table and click Eliminar
then confirm.

TIP: After registering a surgery you can create a
rehabilitation plan for that patient using the AI module
in the Rehabilitacion section.

----------------------------------------------------------------
REHABILITATION
----------------------------------------------------------------

Navigate to: Sidebar -> Rehabilitacion

To add a therapy click Nueva Terapia. Enter the therapy
type such as Fisioterapia or Hidroterapia. Select the start
date and optionally the end date. Choose the progress status
from the following options:

    Iniciado      - therapy just started
    En progreso   - therapy currently ongoing
    Completado    - therapy has been completed
    Suspendido    - therapy was paused or cancelled

Select the patient and click Guardar Terapia.

Each therapy in the table shows a colored progress bar
that reflects the current status visually.

To delete a therapy find it in the table, click Eliminar
and confirm.

To generate an AI rehabilitation plan scroll down to the
dark section at the bottom of the page. This feature
requires an OpenAI API key configured in app.js.

Enter the type of injury or condition in the first field.
Select the activity level of the patient:

    Sedentario         - little or no physical activity
    Actividad leve     - light occasional activity
    Actividad moderada - regular moderate activity
    Muy activo         - intense daily physical activity

Add any additional notes such as adult patient, no gym
access or post-operative if applicable. Then click
Generar Plan con IA and wait a few seconds.

The generated plan will appear with three phases:
Phase 1 covering weeks 1 and 2 with initial gentle
exercises, Phase 2 covering weeks 3 to 6 with progressive
strengthening, and Phase 3 covering week 7 onwards with
functional exercises and return to daily activities.

----------------------------------------------------------------
MEDICATIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------

Navigate to: Sidebar -> Medicamentos

To add a medication click Nuevo Medicamento. Enter the
medication name such as Metformina 500mg. Enter the dosage
such as 500mg or 1 tablet. Select the frequency from
the following options:

    Cada 8 horas
    Cada 12 horas
    Una vez al dia
    Dos veces al dia
    Tres veces al dia
    Segun necesidad

Select the start date and optionally the end date of the
treatment. Select the patient from the dropdown list
and click Guardar.

To delete a medication find it in the table, click Eliminar
and confirm.

----------------------------------------------------------------
CLINICAL HISTORY
----------------------------------------------------------------

Navigate to: Sidebar -> Historial Clinico

The Clinical History page shows a unified view of all
medical records organized in five tabs. Click each tab
to switch between sections:

    Tab 1 - Enfermedades  shows all registered diseases
    Tab 2 - Citas         shows all medical appointments
    Tab 3 - Cirugias      shows all surgeries
    Tab 4 - Terapias      shows all rehabilitation therapies
    Tab 5 - Medicamentos  shows all medications

The statistics bar at the top shows total counts for
diseases, appointments, surgeries and medications.

----------------------------------------------------------------
DIET AND NUTRITION AI MODULE
----------------------------------------------------------------

Navigate to: Sidebar -> Dieta y Nutricion

This feature requires an OpenAI API key configured in
the file js/app.js by the system administrator.

At the top of the page there are quick-select chips for
the most common conditions. Click any chip to automatically
fill the condition field. Available chips include:
Diabetes tipo 2, Hipertension, Hipotiroidismo, Anemia,
Celiaquia, Colon irritable, Obesidad, Colesterol alto,
Insuficiencia renal and Gastritis.

To generate a diet plan enter the medical condition or
click one of the chips. Add any dietary restrictions in
the second field such as vegetariano, sin gluten or
sin lactosa. Select the nutritional goal from:

    Alimentacion equilibrada  - balanced general diet
    Bajar de peso             - weight loss
    Subir de peso             - weight gain
    Control glucemico         - blood sugar control
    Reducir inflamacion       - reduce inflammation

Click Generar Plan de Dieta con IA and wait a few seconds.

The generated plan will include a general description,
a list of recommended foods, a list of foods to avoid,
a full daily meal plan covering breakfast, mid-morning
snack, lunch, afternoon snack and dinner, plus important
clinical tips and medical warnings.

To get a different plan click the Regenerar button that
appears after the plan is displayed.

----------------------------------------------------------------
VIRTUAL ASSISTANT
----------------------------------------------------------------

Navigate to: Sidebar -> Asistente Virtual

This feature requires an OpenAI API key configured in
the file js/app.js by the system administrator.

Type your medical question in the text box at the bottom
of the screen. Press Enter or click the Enviar button.
The assistant will respond within a few seconds. You can
continue the conversation naturally by asking follow-up
questions. To start a completely new conversation click
the button that says Limpiar conversacion at the top.

The virtual assistant can help with general questions
about symptoms, information about medications and their
side effects, nutrition and wellness advice, explanations
of medical terms and general health information.

IMPORTANT: The virtual assistant provides general
information only and does not replace a real doctor.
Always consult a qualified medical professional for
specific diagnoses or treatment decisions.

----------------------------------------------------------------
LOGGING OUT
----------------------------------------------------------------

Click the arrow button at the bottom of the sidebar next
to your name and email to log out. Your session data will
be cleared from the browser and you will be redirected
to the login page automatically.

----------------------------------------------------------------
TROUBLESHOOTING
----------------------------------------------------------------

If the page shows an error or redirects to login
unexpectedly your session has expired. Log in again at
http://localhost:8080/login.html. Sessions last 24 hours.

If the table is empty after adding a record try refreshing
the page. The data was saved correctly to the database.

If the AI modules show an error message the OpenAI API key
is not configured or has no available credits. Contact
the system administrator to resolve this.

If the logo does not appear in the sidebar the file
img/logo.jpeg is missing from the static folder. Ask
the administrator to add it.

If you cannot connect to the system at all the backend
server may not be running. Ask the administrator to
start it using the command mvnw.cmd spring-boot:run
on Windows or ./mvnw spring-boot:run on macOS and Linux.
